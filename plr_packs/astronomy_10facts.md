# 10 Astronomy Facts

Category: Astronomy
Format: Private Label Rights (PLR) micro-pack
Facts: 10

============================================================


### Fact 1
The Sun contains 99.86% of the mass in our solar system.


### Fact 2
A light-year is about 9.46 trillion kilometers (5.88 trillion miles).


### Fact 3
Saturn's rings are made of billions of ice and rock particles.


### Fact 4
There are more stars in the universe than grains of sand on all Earth's beaches.


### Fact 5
The Andromeda Galaxy is on a collision course with the Milky Way.


### Fact 6
A neutron star can spin up to 600 times per second.


### Fact 7
Venus is the hottest planet despite not being closest to the Sun.


### Fact 8
The observable universe is about 93 billion light-years in diameter.


### Fact 9
Black holes have gravitational pulls so strong that not even light escapes.


### Fact 10
Pluto was reclassified as a dwarf planet in 2006.
