# 10 Deep Sea Facts

Category: Deep Sea
Format: Private Label Rights (PLR) micro-pack
Facts: 10

============================================================


### Fact 1
The Mariana Trench is the deepest known point on Earth.


### Fact 2
Hydrothermal vents host life without sunlight.


### Fact 3
The anglerfish glows to attract prey in the dark depths.


### Fact 4
Pressure at depth can crush objects; adapted creatures thrive.


### Fact 5
The deep sea covers more than half of Earth's surface.


### Fact 6
Giant squid live in the deep and have the largest eyes in nature.


### Fact 7
About 80% of the ocean is unexplored and unmapped.


### Fact 8
Deep-sea corals exist at depths over 4,000 meters.


### Fact 9
The dumbo octopus flaps 'ears' to swim in the abyss.


### Fact 10
Some trenches contain ecosystems isolated for millennia.
