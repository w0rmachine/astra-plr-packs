# 10 Robotics Facts

Category: Robotics
Format: Private Label Rights (PLR) micro-pack
Facts: 10

============================================================


### Fact 1
The word 'robot' comes from the Czech word 'robota' meaning forced labor.


### Fact 2
Industrial robots move along six or more axes of motion.


### Fact 3
The first industrial robot, Unimate, began work in 1961.


### Fact 4
Humanoid robot Sophia was granted citizenship by Saudi Arabia in 2017.


### Fact 5
Swarm robots coordinate like insects to complete tasks.


### Fact 6
Robotic surgical systems enable minimally invasive procedures.


### Fact 7
Boston Dynamics' Spot walks with four legs on rough terrain.


### Fact 8
Robots are used to defuse bombs, reducing human risk.


### Fact 9
Agricultural robots weed, harvest, and monitor crops.


### Fact 10
Reinforcement learning lets robots learn by trial and error.
