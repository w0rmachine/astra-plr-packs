# 10 Space Exploration Facts

Category: Space Exploration
Format: Private Label Rights (PLR) micro-pack
Facts: 10

============================================================


### Fact 1
The first human in space was Yuri Gagarin in 1961.


### Fact 2
Apollo 11 landed humans on the Moon in 1969.


### Fact 3
Voyager 1 is the most distant human-made object from Earth.


### Fact 4
The International Space Station orbits Earth every 90 minutes.


### Fact 5
Mars rovers have been exploring the Red Planet since 1997.


### Fact 6
The Hubble Space Telescope has observed over 30,000 celestial objects.


### Fact 7
SpaceX's Falcon Heavy is among the most powerful operational rockets.


### Fact 8
Artemis aims to return humans to the Moon in the 2020s.


### Fact 9
The James Webb Space Telescope launched in December 2021.


### Fact 10
Astronauts on the ISS see 16 sunrises and sunsets each day.
