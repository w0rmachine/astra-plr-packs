# 10 Renewable Energy Facts

Category: Renewable Energy
Format: Private Label Rights (PLR) micro-pack
Facts: 10

============================================================


### Fact 1
Solar energy is the most abundant energy source on Earth.


### Fact 2
Wind turbines convert kinetic energy from air flow into electricity.


### Fact 3
Hydropower currently produces more electricity than all other renewables combined.


### Fact 4
Geothermal energy taps heat from within the Earth's crust.


### Fact 5
Denmark generated over 50% of its electricity from wind power in recent years.


### Fact 6
Solar panels have become about 90% cheaper in the last decade.


### Fact 7
Renewable energy sources emit little to no greenhouse gases.


### Fact 8
Battery storage helps smooth intermittent solar and wind generation.


### Fact 9
Tidal energy harnesses the predictable rise and fall of ocean tides.


### Fact 10
Nearly 30% of global electricity is now generated by renewables.
